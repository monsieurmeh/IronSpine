# Iron Spine: The First Custom Feat

I hate carrying stuff, and look for every opportunity to ease increase capacity. None of the vanilla feats help with this, and my **poor back**, so... Iron Spine!

Adjustable in ModSettings, both the bonus (+3kg) and the requirement (10,000 kg * km traveled) are geared towards long, encumbered journeys. Every tick, the mod will measure how far you have traveled and add that X your current carry weight towards your progress counter.

Current artwork from Kaiusername, with a final version from Flower Field on route.
